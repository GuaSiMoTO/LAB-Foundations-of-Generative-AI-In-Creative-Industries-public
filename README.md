# LAB - Reboot AI Contest

## Introduction

Welcome to the **Reboot AI Contest**, a creative competition designed for students to showcase their skills in various AI-driven challenges. Participants will form teams of 3-4 members and compete in four exciting categories, each focusing on different applications of generative AI.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dbjlaop5w/image/upload/v1730206364/codeskills/generative-ai/Generative%20AI%20in%20Creative%20Industries/ux3dnavdypiaw78sjb7n.jpg)

> ***Note:*** _This is a group activity aimed at fostering teamwork and creativity in the use of AI tools._

## Requirements

- Knowledge in basic AI tools and platforms (Suno, ChatGPT, SumerMeme, InVideo)
- A GitHub account to fork and clone this repository
- Collaborative spirit and creative approach to each challenge!

## Contest Modalities

### 1. The Summer Song
Participants will use AI-powered music generation tools like **Suno** to create a short song.


![ChatGPT Image 27 jun 2025, 15_21_27](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5e1ad63c-0cd8-4d81-8b03-f17283547cec)

- **Duration:** Up to 1 minute
- **Theme:** Free choice
  
### 2. Meme Contest
Using tools like **SumerMeme.ai**, teams will create a humorous meme.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dbjlaop5w/image/upload/v1730211512/codeskills/generative-ai/Generative%20AI%20in%20Creative%20Industries/mtjqjqpecz8rpc4i2oqy.jpg)

- **Theme:** Programming

### 3. Storyboard Challenge
With the help of **ChatGPT**, teams will recreate the following scene:

> A programmer is frustrated because their code isn't working. To unblock, they begin talking to their rubber duck, and suddenly they realize the error. The programmer fixes the bug and becomes ecstatic. The scene ends with a close-up of the inanimate duck, building suspense.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dbjlaop5w/image/upload/v1730211014/codeskills/generative-ai/Generative%20AI%20in%20Creative%20Industries/yrz0b3ykswleoempod98.jpg)

- **Number of Frames:** Between 5 and 8

>**Tip:** Use AI tools to refine your prompts and improve scene details.

### 4. Movie Trailer
Using **InVideo**, teams will create a suspenseful movie trailer.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dbjlaop5w/image/upload/v1730211130/codeskills/generative-ai/Generative%20AI%20in%20Creative%20Industries/k5beqwkz5l5mjcjaq6hl.webp)

- **Duration:** 35 seconds
- **Theme:** Suspense

## Additional Guidelines

- **Teamwork:** Collaborate effectively within your team to divide tasks based on each member's strengths.
- **Tool Proficiency:** Practice using each tool in advance to maximize quality.
- **Originality:** All creations must be original and created during the contest period.

## Submission

1. **GitHub Repository:** Each team should fork this repository to work on their project and commit their final versions to their repository.
2. **Final Presentation:** Each team will present their work at the end of the contest. Be ready to explain your approach, challenges, and outcomes.
